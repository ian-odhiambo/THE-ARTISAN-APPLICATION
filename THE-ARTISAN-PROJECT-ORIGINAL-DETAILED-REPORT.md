## CHAPTER ONE: INTRODUCTION (2-3 pages)

The Artisan Marketplace was developed to bridge the digital gap for Kenyan artisans by providing a comprehensive e-commerce platform featuring product moderation, category-based discovery with artisan-specific carousels, and role-based dashboards. Implemented using the MERN stack with modern frontend practices (Vite, Tailwind CSS, React Context), the system facilitated seamless artisan product uploads, administrative approval workflows, and customer browsing experiences optimized for mobile and desktop.
